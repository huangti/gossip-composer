# Visual Metaphor Examples

These are concepts developed for prior market-commentary illustrations. They are **examples of reasoning**, not reusable defaults.

1. **Packed steam train, nearly empty coal tender**: A celebrated full train and bustling departure contrast with the small remaining fuel supply. Use to communicate strong present momentum versus uncertain resources for continuation.
2. **Grand hotel installing new locks on every door**: A locksmith celebrates a huge replacement order while an apprentice notices the doors are already fitted. Use to communicate a replacement boom that may not repeat.
3. **Tailor adding pockets to an overloaded coat**: A banker-tailor keeps adding pockets to one customer's coat while an empty rack waits. Use to contrast monetizing existing customers with acquiring new ones.
4. **Busy industrial electrical counter, quiet household counter**: Corporate buyers queue for large equipment while a household faces a towering stack of loan papers. Use to contrast demand conditions across customer segments.

For each new article, extract the **actual** tension first. Do not use these examples if they distort the user's point.
