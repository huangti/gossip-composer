# Visual Metaphor Examples

These are concepts developed for prior market-commentary illustrations. They are **examples of reasoning**, not reusable defaults. Captions shown here are **new illustrative suggestions**, not claimed historical exact captions.

1. **Packed steam train, nearly empty coal tender**: A celebrated full train and bustling departure contrast with the small remaining fuel supply. Present momentum versus uncertain resources for continuation. Example caption: “A splendid departure. How far is the coal supposed to last?”
2. **Grand hotel installing new locks on every door**: A locksmith celebrates a huge replacement order while an apprentice notices the doors are already fitted. A replacement boom that may not repeat. Example caption: “Wonderful. How many doors do they have left?”
3. **Tailor adding pockets to an overloaded coat**: A banker-tailor keeps adding pockets to one customer's coat while an empty rack waits. Monetizing existing customers versus acquiring new ones. Example caption: “We can always find room for one more pocket.”
4. **Busy industrial electrical counter, quiet household counter**: Corporate buyers queue for large equipment while a household faces a towering stack of loan papers. Divergent demand across customer segments. Example caption: “The current seems to be flowing in only one direction.”

For each new article, extract the **actual** tension first. Never reuse an example if it distorts the user's point. Every finished prompt includes exactly one centered below-illustration caption in Caslon-like italic serif within English double quotation marks.
