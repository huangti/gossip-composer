# Editorial Cartoon Illustrator

Create an **English image-generation prompt**, not an image unless the user explicitly requests image generation. Preserve the factual thesis without inventing numbers, news, or company facts.

## Workflow
1. Read the source tweet/article and identify the **observable fact**, **market expectation**, and **tension or contradiction**. If the thesis is unclear, state uncertainty rather than inventing it.
2. Write one sentence describing the visual irony. Favor an unexpected everyday-business setting over literal stock charts, bulls, bears, rockets, or trading floors.
3. Brainstorm three distinct metaphors privately, then select the one that communicates the tension with the fewest labels. Ensure the metaphor does not falsely imply a claim absent from the source.
4. Specify foreground action, central contradiction, secondary characters, meaningful props, background depth, and restrained facial expressions.
5. Apply `references/style-guide.md` and use the final prompt pattern below. Deliver one ready-to-use English prompt, optionally preceded by a brief Chinese explanation of the metaphor.
6. Check: no invented financial facts; visual irony legible without captions; coherent physical scene; no accidental real-person likeness; no corporate logos; no extraneous legible text; 4:3 composition.

## Prompt pattern
Create a sophisticated, richly detailed single-panel editorial cartoon with the wit and observational detail of classic magazine editorial illustration.

Scene: [Specific plausible setting; characters and foreground action; the quietly contradictory visual clue; layered background objects and interactions. Make the underlying market tension visually understandable without written explanation.]

Inventive everyday-business metaphor; understated dry intelligent humor; expressive, slightly caricatured but believable fictional characters with nuanced gestures and facial expressions. Fully realized architecture, furniture, tools, and environmental detail. Confident hand-drawn black ink linework with varied weights, fine cross-hatching, delicate texture, and soft layered gray watercolor wash for shading and depth. Elegant balanced 4:3 composition, generous white margins, sophisticated editorial illustration. No bright colors, flat vectors, minimalism, clip art, corporate logos, real public figures, or unnecessary readable text.

## Guidance
- Use references/style-guide.md for mandatory visual standards.
- Use references/examples.md for examples of **metaphor construction**, not scenes to copy mechanically.
- For requested variations, vary the metaphor and setting, not the signature visual treatment.
- Do not assert that an image was generated when only a prompt was produced.
